How often should water tanks be cleaned in Qatar?

22 November 2026 · 3 min read · By the JAFM team

Short answer

Clean and disinfect your water tank at least once a year. For homes, this is not only good practice: KAHRAMAA's Water Installations Code says the customer must maintain and clean water storage tanks in residential premises annually. Busy buildings such as schools, clinics and staff accommodation are better served by a clean every six months.

Why it matters more in Qatar

Most homes and buildings in Qatar store water in a roof tank, a ground tank or both before it reaches the taps. That water can sit for days in high heat.

KAHRAMAA's own water quality guidance points to three local risks:

  • Windblown dust can get into storage tanks and make the water cloudy.
  • Roof tanks can raise the temperature of the water.
  • Where sunlight reaches roof and ground tanks, algae and biofilm can grow.

Sand and sediment also settle on the tank floor over the months. A tank can look fine from the outside and still need cleaning inside.

How often, by building type

BuildingHow oftenNotes
Villa or private homeAt least once a yearAnnual cleaning is required for residential tanks under KAHRAMAA's code
Apartment building or compoundAt least once a yearConsider twice a year for large buildings or older tanks
Office or commercial buildingAt least once a yearMore often if the tank is old or exposed to sun
School, nursery or clinicEvery six monthsMany people use the water every day
Restaurant, hotel or staff accommodationEvery six monthsHigh water use and food preparation
After repairs, new pipework or a long empty periodBefore the water is used againClean and disinfect before people move back in

If you do not know when your tank was last cleaned, treat it as due now.

Signs your tank needs cleaning sooner

  • Cloudy or discoloured water
  • A musty or unusual smell or taste
  • Sand or grit in sinks, showers or filters
  • Stains on fixtures or white clothes
  • A cracked, loose or missing lid or vent cover

What a proper clean should include

  • Inspection of the tank, lid, vents and inlet and outlet pipes
  • Draining the tank and removing sludge and sediment
  • Scrubbing the walls, floor and ceiling of the tank
  • Disinfecting the inside with a product suitable for water tanks
  • Rinsing well so no cleaning residue is left
  • Refilling and a final visual check
  • A short written report on what was found

Between cleans, keep the tank area locked and check the tank regularly. KAHRAMAA's code asks for both.

Frequently asked questions

Is water tank cleaning a legal requirement in Qatar?

For homes, yes. KAHRAMAA's Water Installations Code (2016) says that the customer has to maintain and clean water storage tanks in residential premises annually. For other buildings, once a year is the common minimum.

Who is responsible for cleaning the tank?

Under the code, the customer is responsible for residential tanks. In rented property, check your lease to see whether the landlord or the tenant arranges it.

How long does a tank clean take?

A single villa tank usually takes a few hours. Large buildings with several tanks, or big underground tanks, take longer.

Will we be without water during the clean?

Yes, for a short time, because the tank has to be drained. Agree the timing in advance and plan for an early morning or a quieter day.
