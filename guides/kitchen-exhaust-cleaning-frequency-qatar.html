Kitchen exhaust cleaning for restaurants in Qatar: how often, and why it matters for fire safety

24 November 2026 · 4 min read · By the JAFM team

Short answer

Most restaurant kitchens in Qatar should have the whole exhaust system (hood, filters, ducts and fan) inspected and cleaned every six months. Busy high-volume kitchens need it every three months, and kitchens that cook over charcoal or wood every month. Small kitchens with light cooking can go up to a year. These intervals come from NFPA 96, the widely used international standard for commercial kitchen ventilation and fire protection. Hood filters need cleaning much more often, usually by your own kitchen team.

Why it matters more in Qatar

Cooking sends grease-laden vapour into the hood. Filters catch some of it. The rest sticks inside the hood, the ducts and the fan. Grease burns easily. A flare-up on the grill can reach a greasy duct, and a duct fire spreads fast and is hard to reach.

Kitchens in Qatar face extra pressure:

  • Long hours. Many restaurants open from breakfast until late, and some never close.
  • High-volume grilling and frying. Mixed grills, shawarma, fried snacks and wok dishes all put a lot of grease into the air.
  • Heat. Kitchens are already hot, and summer temperatures make the ventilation work harder. A greasy system moves less air, so the kitchen gets hotter and smokier.

Fire safety is also part of licensing. For example, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry’s guide for cloud kitchens lists approval from the Civil Defence Department at the Ministry of Interior, approval from the Ministry of Municipality, and compliance with civil defence, fire prevention and industrial ventilation requirements.

How often, by kitchen type

Kitchen typeExamplesInspect and clean the full system
Solid fuelCharcoal grills, charcoal tandoors, wood-fired ovensEvery month
High volume24-hour kitchens, charbroiling, wok cooking, very busy grill and fried-food outletsEvery 3 months
Moderate volumeMost restaurants, hotel and banquet kitchens, staff canteensEvery 6 months
Low volumeSmall cafés, light-use pantries, seasonal kitchensEvery 12 months

NFPA 96 sets these as intervals for inspecting for grease build-up, and any part with build-up must then be cleaned. In a busy Qatar kitchen there is almost always build-up by then, so plan a clean at the same interval. If you are unsure which row fits, choose the more frequent one.

What a proper clean covers

A hood-only wipe is not enough. The hidden grease in the ducts matters most. A proper clean should cover:

  • Covering cooking equipment and nearby surfaces before work starts
  • Filters removed and fully degreased
  • The hood canopy, inside and out
  • Ductwork cleaned through access panels, along the run to the fan
  • The exhaust fan housing and blades
  • Access panels checked and refitted properly
  • The work area cleaned up afterwards
  • A report with before-and-after photos, the date, and any areas that could not be reached

Under NFPA 96, the cleaning company should leave a dated certificate and report any areas it could not reach or clean.

Signs your exhaust is overdue

  • Grease dripping from the hood edges or filters
  • Filters that still feel sticky after washing
  • Smoke or cooking smells that hang around
  • The kitchen feels hotter and the extraction feels weak
  • The fan is noisier than usual or vibrates
  • No one can remember the last full clean

Frequently asked questions

Is kitchen exhaust cleaning required in Qatar?

Civil Defence approval is part of licensing food businesses such as cloud kitchens. For the cleaning schedule itself, the widely used reference is NFPA 96. Your fire safety consultant can confirm what applies to your site.

Can our kitchen staff clean the hood themselves?

Your team should clean the filters and the outside of the hood regularly. Ducts and fans are different: NFPA 96 says the system should be cleaned by properly trained and qualified people.

Do we need to close the kitchen during the clean?

Cooking has to stop while the system is being cleaned. Most kitchens book the work overnight, early in the morning or on a closed day.
