How we work

From the first visit to everyday service, you always know what's happening.

Site visit

We visit your building and understand how it is used.

Tailored proposal

A clear scope, schedule and price for your site.

Team mobilised

Trained staff start on an agreed date.

Quality checks

Regular inspections and reporting keep standards high.

Five levels of quality checks

Standards are checked at every level, from the person doing the work to a joint audit with you.

Self-check

Every operative checks their own work against the agreed standard.

Shift check

Work is checked every shift, by the site supervisor where the contract includes one.

Weekly audit

A scored audit of the site every week.

Monthly joint audit

We walk the site with you every month and agree any actions.

Quarterly audit

An internal audit every quarter, plus an unannounced audit each year.
