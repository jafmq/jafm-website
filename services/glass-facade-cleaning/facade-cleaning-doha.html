Glass & Facade Cleaning

Facade Cleaning in Doha and Across Qatar

A building's facade is the first thing tenants, visitors and clients see. In Qatar, dust, sand, humidity and hard water leave a film on glass, cladding and stone within weeks. JAFM Qatar provides facade cleaning in Doha and across Qatar for office towers, hotels, residential buildings, malls and government buildings. We have worked in facility management since 1977 and hold ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification, certified by TNV.

Why facades get dirty so fast in Qatar

Fine desert dust settles on every surface, and the morning humidity turns it into a sticky layer that bakes on in the sun. A single sandstorm can coat a whole elevation in a day. Hard water from irrigation and earlier cleaning leaves white spots on glass, and traffic fumes, bird droppings and AC condensate add streaks.

Left alone, this does more than look bad. Some deposits etch glass over time, and dirty stone and cladding can stain for good. Regular cleaning protects the facade and is a chance to spot damaged seals, loose panels or cracked glass early.

What our facade cleaning covers

  • Curtain walls and external windows
  • Aluminium composite panels (ACP) and metal cladding
  • Natural stone, GRC and precast concrete
  • Glass canopies, skylights and atrium roofs
  • Louvres, fins, sunshades and balconies
  • Signage and building lettering
  • Hard-water stains, bird droppings and streaks, where the surface allows

How we reach every part of the building

Every facade is different, so the access method is chosen after a survey, elevation by elevation:

  • Water-fed poles with purified water for ground-floor and low-level glass
  • Mobile elevating work platforms (MEWP) for mid-rise areas, canopies and parts a BMU cannot reach
  • The building's BMU or gondola for towers that have one, once we have checked it is certified and safe to use
  • Rope access for high-rise elevations, curved or recessed areas and buildings without a BMU

All work at height follows a written method statement and rescue plan. The area below is barriered with a marshal, tools are tethered, and work stops if wind, sandstorms, rain or lightning make it unsafe.

How it works, step by step

  1. Request a quote. Tell us about the building, its height and what you want cleaned.
  2. Site survey. We check the glass and cladding, anchor points, any BMU, access and restrictions.
  3. Written quote and method statement. You receive the price, the access method for each elevation and the safety plan.
  4. Schedule. We plan around your tenants and opening hours, often early mornings or weekends for busy entrances.
  5. Cleaning. Top to bottom, elevation by elevation, with a supervisor on site.
  6. Handover. We walk the finished elevations with you and report anything that needs repair, such as damaged seals or cracked glass.

How often should a facade be cleaned in Qatar?

It depends on where the building is, how tall it is and how it is used. As a guide:

Hotels, prestige office towers and showroomsEvery 3 to 4 months
Most commercial and residential buildingsEvery 5 to 6 months
Stone and claddingOnce or twice a year, with spot cleaning in between
After a major sandstormA one-off clean where appearance matters

Buildings near the coast, main roads or construction sites usually need cleaning more often. October to April is the busiest season, because the cooler weather suits work at height, so it helps to book early.

Frequently asked questions

Can you clean a building that has no BMU?

Yes. Without a BMU we use rope access, mobile elevating work platforms or water-fed poles, depending on the height and layout of the building.

Do you work early mornings or at weekends?

Yes, where the building allows it. Entrances and busy elevations are often cleaned early in the morning or at weekends to keep disruption low.

Will cleaning damage the glass or cladding?

No. We use purified water and cleaning products suited to each surface, and avoid abrasive tools on coated glass and cladding.

Can you remove hard-water stains?

Light stains usually come off during cleaning. Heavy or old stains may need a separate restoration treatment, which we can quote for after testing a small area.

Do you offer annual facade cleaning contracts?

Yes. A contract with a fixed number of cleans per year spreads the cost and keeps the building looking the same all year.
