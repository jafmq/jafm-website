Pest control services in Qatar

Pests are a health risk, a reputational risk and, in food and healthcare settings, a compliance risk. We use integrated pest management: we find the cause, remove it and use pesticide only where it is needed.

What's included

  • Cockroaches and other crawling insects
  • Rodents, with locked and numbered bait stations
  • Flying insects, mosquitoes and flies
  • Termites
  • Bird proofing with nets, spikes and wires
  • Gel baiting and crack-and-crevice treatment
  • Insect light traps and glue boards
  • Fogging treatments where needed

How we deliver

  • Inspection first, with proofing gaps reported to you in writing
  • Physical control first, and pesticide only where needed
  • 24-hour written notice before spraying, with bilingual signs
  • Treatment records, site maps and a monthly review with you

How it works

  1. Inspection. We inspect the site to find where pests are coming from, where they hide and what attracts them, and report proofing gaps to you in writing.
  2. Treatment plan. We agree the treatments, the bait station layout and the visit schedule with you, using physical control first.
  3. Treatment. Treatments are carried out with notice to your staff and clear signs, at times that suit your operation.
  4. Monitoring and review. Bait stations and traps are checked on every visit. Each visit is recorded on a site map, and we review activity with you every month.

Pest control for food, healthcare and offices

  • Restaurants, kitchens and food stores: gel baits and crack-and-crevice treatment that keep products away from food surfaces, with records ready for inspections.
  • Hospitals and clinics: low-impact methods, and treatments timed around patient areas and infection control rules.
  • Offices, schools and residential buildings: regular preventive visits, plus call-outs when there is a problem.

Common pests in Qatar

Cockroaches, rats and mice, ants, bed bugs, flies, mosquitoes, termites and pigeons. Each needs a different treatment, so we identify the pest before treating it.

Frequently asked questions

Is pest control safe around staff and children?

We use pesticide only where it is needed and choose baits and targeted treatments over spraying. When spraying is needed, we give 24 hours' written notice and put up bilingual signs.

How often should pest control be done?

Most offices and residential buildings need a monthly visit. Restaurants, kitchens and food stores often need visits every two weeks.

Do I need to leave the building during treatment?

For gel baits and traps, no. For spraying or fogging, the treated area is closed for a short time, and we tell you in advance how long.

Do you get rid of pigeons?

Yes. We use bird proofing such as nets, spikes and wires to keep pigeons off ledges, roofs and air conditioning units without harming them.

Do you keep records for inspections?

Yes. Every visit is recorded with the treatments used and a site map of bait stations, which you can show to inspectors and auditors.
