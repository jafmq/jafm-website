Glass & facade cleaning

Clean glass and facades are the first thing visitors see. We clean everything from internal partitions to high-rise curtain walls, choosing the safest access method for each part of your building.

What's included

  • Internal glass, partitions and balustrades
  • Atrium and shopfront glazing
  • External windows and curtain walls
  • Skylights, glazed canopies and roof lights
  • Signage, cladding, stone and other facade surfaces
  • Access from the ground with water-fed poles and purified water
  • Mobile elevating work platforms (MEWP)
  • The building's BMU or gondola
  • Rope access

How we deliver

  • Barriered exclusion zones with a marshal below the work
  • All tools tethered at height
  • A rescue plan in place for work at height
  • Wind checked at working height, and work stops for sandstorms, rain or lightning

How it works

  1. Survey. We look at the building's height, glass type, cladding, anchor points and any BMU or gondola, and agree which areas can be reached from inside, from the ground or from height.
  2. Method statement. We choose the safest access for each elevation and set out the method, the exclusion zones and the rescue plan in writing before work starts.
  3. Cleaning. Work is planned around your opening hours and the weather, with barriers and a marshal below.
  4. Handover. We walk the finished areas with you and note anything that needs a return visit, such as hard-water stains or damaged seals.

How often to clean glass and facades in Qatar

Dust and sand settle on glass quickly in Qatar, so most buildings need more frequent cleaning than in other climates:

  • Internal glass and entrance doors: daily or weekly
  • Shopfronts and ground-floor glazing: weekly
  • External windows and curtain walls: every 1–3 months
  • Facades, cladding and stone: once or twice a year
  • After a sandstorm: a one-off clean when visibility matters, for example for hotels and showrooms

Buildings we clean

Office towers, hotels, hospitals, malls and showrooms, government buildings, schools, residential towers and villas.

Frequently asked questions

How much does window cleaning cost in Qatar?

It depends on the glass area, the building height and the access method needed. We offer a free survey and a written quote.

Can you clean high-rise buildings?

Yes. High-rise glass and facades are cleaned using the building's BMU or gondola, mobile elevating work platforms or rope access, depending on the building.

Do you use chemicals on the glass?

For most glass we use purified water, which dries without spots. Where needed we use a mild, glass-safe cleaner suited to coated or tinted glass.

What happens in bad weather?

We check the wind at working height before and during work, and stop for sandstorms, rain or lightning. Work is rescheduled with you.

Do you clean facade cladding and stone, as well as glass?

Yes. We clean aluminium cladding, signage, stone and other facade surfaces, choosing a method that won't damage the finish.
