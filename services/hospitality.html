Office support & hospitality staff

The right support staff keep an office running smoothly and make a good impression on your visitors. We provide trained, well-presented staff for front-of-house and back-office roles, and we manage their attendance and performance.

What's included

  • Tea and pantry staff, office assistants and waiters
  • Reception staff
  • Event staff for conferences and functions
  • Store, filing and records staff, and general helpers
  • Female staff for the ladies' majlis on request
  • Desk tea rounds at times agreed with you
  • Meeting room set-up and service
  • Arabic coffee, majlis and VIP service
  • Events and buffet service planned with you
  • Water delivery to every floor, and pantry stock lists sent to you

How we deliver

  • Discretion: staff never read, move or photograph documents or screens
  • A clean uniform every shift, with grooming checked every shift
  • Food handlers hold the MoPH food handler permit
  • New staff work alongside an experienced colleague until signed off

How it works

  1. Requirements. We agree the roles, numbers, working hours, dress code and language needs with you.
  2. Selection. We put forward staff with the right experience and presentation for your office, and you can meet them before they start.
  3. Induction. Each person is briefed on your premises, your routines, your guests and your rules on confidentiality.
  4. Ongoing management. We handle attendance, uniforms and performance, and arrange cover for leave and absence, so you deal with one point of contact.

Where our staff work

Corporate head offices, government ministries and authorities, banks, embassies, hospitals, schools and universities, and conferences and events.

A typical day for a tea and pantry team

  • Pantry opened, cleaned and stocked before staff arrive
  • Morning tea and coffee rounds to desks and offices
  • Meeting rooms set up, served and cleared between meetings
  • Majlis and VIP guests served with Arabic coffee and dates
  • Afternoon rounds, pantry cleaned and a stock list sent to you

Frequently asked questions

Can I hire staff for a single event?

Yes. We provide staff for one-off conferences, functions and VIP visits, as well as long-term placements.

Do you provide female staff?

Yes. Female staff are available on request, for example for a ladies' majlis or women's sections.

What happens if a staff member is absent?

We arrange cover so your office keeps running.

Are your food handlers certified?

Yes. Staff who handle food hold the Ministry of Public Health food handler permit.

How do you protect confidential information?

Staff are trained never to read, move or photograph documents or screens, and we can sign a confidentiality agreement with you.
