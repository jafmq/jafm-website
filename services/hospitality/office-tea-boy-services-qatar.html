Office Support & Hospitality Staff

Office Tea Boy and Pantry Staff Services in Qatar

Tea and coffee service is part of office life in Qatar. A good tea and pantry team, often called tea boys, keeps staff refreshed, looks after visitors and makes every meeting run smoothly. JAFM Qatar provides trained, well-presented tea and pantry staff to offices, government buildings, banks and embassies across Qatar. We have worked in facility management since 1977 and hold ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification, certified by TNV.

What our tea and pantry staff do

  • Morning and afternoon tea and coffee rounds to desks and offices
  • Arabic coffee, dates and majlis service for guests
  • Meeting room set-up, service during meetings and clearing afterwards
  • VIP and guest service at reception and in executive offices
  • Keeping the pantry clean, organised and stocked
  • Washing cups, glasses and kitchen equipment
  • Water delivery to every floor
  • Sending you stock lists so supplies never run out
  • Support at internal events, buffets and visits

The standards we keep

  • Presentation: a clean uniform every shift, with grooming checked
  • Food hygiene: staff who handle food hold the MoPH food handler permit
  • Discretion: staff never read, move or photograph documents or screens
  • Training: new staff work alongside an experienced colleague until signed off
  • Cover: leave and absence are covered, so service does not stop

How it works, step by step

  1. Tell us what you need. The number of staff, working hours, floors, dress code and language needs.
  2. Selection. We put forward staff with the right experience and presentation, and you can meet them before they start.
  3. Induction. Each person is briefed on your office, your routines, your guests and your rules on confidentiality.
  4. Start of service. The team starts on an agreed date, with a supervisor checking in during the first weeks.
  5. Ongoing management. We handle attendance, uniforms, performance and cover, so you have one point of contact.

A typical day

  • Pantry opened, cleaned and stocked before staff arrive
  • Morning tea and coffee rounds
  • Meeting rooms set up, served and cleared between meetings
  • Guests and VIPs served with Arabic coffee and dates
  • Afternoon rounds, pantry cleaned and a stock list sent to you

How many staff do you need?

It depends on how many people you have, how many floors, how often tea rounds are served, and how many meetings and visitors you receive each day. Executive floors, majlis and busy reception areas usually need their own dedicated staff. We visit your office, look at your routine and recommend the right number, so service stays quick without paying for more staff than you need.

Frequently asked questions

Can we get staff for a single event?

Yes. We provide staff for one-off conferences, functions and VIP visits, as well as long-term placements.

Do you provide female staff?

Yes. Female staff are available on request, for example for a ladies' majlis or women's sections.

What happens if a staff member is on leave or absent?

We arrange cover, so the service continues without a gap.

Can we interview staff before they start?

Yes. You can meet the staff we put forward and choose who works in your office.

Can tea and pantry staff be part of a cleaning contract?

Yes. Many clients combine tea and pantry staff with cleaning and other services under one contract, with one point of contact.
