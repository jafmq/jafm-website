Commercial & residential cleaning in Qatar

Clean buildings make a better impression, a healthier workplace and a longer-lasting property. Our trained teams keep every area to an agreed standard, every day, and carry out heavier work out of hours so your building keeps running.

What's included

  • Daily cleaning of entrances, corridors, lifts and stairs
  • Washrooms, including refilling dispensers and emptying sanitary and waste bins
  • Offices, desks, meeting rooms and pantries
  • Common areas and car parks, including litter picking and oil stain treatment
  • Bin rooms and refuse chutes: pressure washing and disinfection
  • Periodic floor care: scrubbing, stripping, sealing and polishing
  • Marble and stone cleaned with low water flow and neutral cleaners
  • Carpet care with vacuuming, spot treatment and hot-water extraction
  • Deep cleaning by a dedicated crew, finished with a joint inspection with you

How we deliver

  • Colour-coded equipment: red for toilets, yellow for washbasins, blue for general areas and green for kitchens, or your own scheme
  • Microfibre cloths, flat mops and chemical dosing units to cut water and chemical use
  • The least hazardous product that does the job
  • Heavier work such as floor stripping and carpet extraction done out of hours or at weekends

How it works

  1. Site visit. We walk the building with you, measure the areas and note floor types, washrooms, traffic and any special requirements.
  2. Scope and schedule. We agree which tasks are done daily, weekly, monthly and periodically, and at what times, so cleaning fits around your working hours.
  3. Mobilisation. The team is briefed on your site, your colour-coding scheme and your safety rules before the first shift.
  4. Checks and review. Work is inspected against the agreed scope, and any issue you raise is logged and closed out. We review the schedule with you as your needs change.

Buildings we clean

Offices and corporate headquarters, government buildings, schools and universities, hospitals and clinics, banks, residential towers and compounds, villas, retail and showrooms, and industrial and warehouse facilities across Qatar.

Frequently asked questions

How much does office cleaning cost in Qatar?

It depends on the size of the building, the number of washrooms, how often each area is cleaned and the hours you need. We offer a free site visit and a written quote based on your actual scope.

Do you offer one-off deep cleaning?

Yes. We carry out one-off deep cleans for move-ins, move-outs, handovers after fit-out, and before events or inspections, as well as regular contracts.

Can cleaning be done outside working hours?

Yes. Daily cleaning can be scheduled early in the morning, during the day or in the evening, and heavier work such as floor stripping is usually done out of hours or at weekends.

What cleaning products do you use?

We choose the least hazardous product that does the job, use dosing units to control quantities, and can work with your preferred or eco-friendly products on request.

Do you clean villas and residential buildings?

Yes. We clean residential towers, compounds and villas, including common areas, car parks and bin rooms, as well as individual units.
