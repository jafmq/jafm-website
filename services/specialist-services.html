Water tank cleaning

Stored water can collect sediment, algae and bacteria, especially in Qatar's heat. The tank is drained, the sediment removed, and the walls scrubbed and disinfected. It is then rinsed and refilled, and a water sample goes to a laboratory, with the results shared with you. Most buildings clean their tanks every six months.

Kitchen exhaust hood and duct cleaning

Grease builds up inside hoods, filters and ducts and is a common cause of kitchen fires. We degrease the hood, filters, ductwork and fan, and give you a record of the work for your insurer and civil defence inspections. Busy commercial kitchens usually need this every three to six months.

Marble, carpet and washroom care

  • Marble polishing and restoration: grinding out scratches, honing and polishing to bring back the shine, then sealing to protect the stone.
  • Deep carpet cleaning: hot-water extraction to remove embedded dirt and stains, with drying times planned around your use of the space.
  • Washroom hygiene: sanitary bins, air fresheners and hand dryers supplied, serviced and replaced on a fixed schedule.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a water tank be cleaned in Qatar?

We recommend at least every six months, and sooner if the water looks or smells different.

Is the water tested after cleaning?

Yes. A sample is sent to a laboratory after cleaning and disinfection, and the results are shared with you.

Will the kitchen have to close during exhaust cleaning?

The work is usually done after closing time or overnight, so the kitchen can open as normal the next day.

Can scratched or dull marble be restored?

In most cases, yes. Grinding, honing and polishing removes light scratches and etching and brings back the shine.

Can I book these services without a full cleaning contract?

Yes. Each service can be booked as a one-off job or added to a regular cleaning or facility management contract.
