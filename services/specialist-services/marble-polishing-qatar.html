Marble Polishing and Restoration in Doha and Across Qatar

Marble floors are part of many buildings in Qatar, from hotel lobbies and office receptions to villas and mosques. Over time, foot traffic, sand and the wrong cleaning products leave marble dull, scratched and stained. JAFM Qatar provides marble polishing and restoration in Doha and across Qatar. We have worked in facility management since 1977 and hold ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification, certified by TNV.

Why marble loses its shine in Qatar

Sand is the biggest enemy of marble floors. Fine grains carried in on shoes act like sandpaper and wear away the polished surface, especially at entrances, lifts and corridors. Acidic cleaners, spilled juice and coffee, and many general detergents etch the stone and leave dull patches. Water can leave rings and yellowing, and trolleys or heavy furniture can chip edges.

Most dull marble does not need replacing. The right treatment can usually bring back the shine for a fraction of the cost of new stone.

Which treatment does your floor need?

We look at the floor first and recommend the lightest treatment that will work:

  • Cleaning and buffing for floors that are dull but not scratched
  • Polishing or crystallisation to bring back the shine on worn but even floors
  • Honing to remove light scratches, etching and water marks
  • Diamond grinding to remove deep scratches and level uneven tiles, followed by honing and polishing
  • Sealing to protect the stone against stains once it is polished

We also treat granite, terrazzo and other natural stone on floors, walls, stairs and counters.

What our marble polishing includes

  • Inspection of the stone, and a small test area where needed
  • Moving light furniture and protecting walls, skirting and nearby surfaces
  • Deep cleaning to remove dirt and old wax

  • Treating stains where the stone allows

How often should marble be polished?

Hotel lobbies, malls and busy receptionsPolishing every 1 to 3 months, with regular buffing
Offices and residential building lobbiesEvery 3 to 6 months
VillasAbout once a year, or when the floor starts to look dull
GrindingOnly for deep scratches or uneven tiles, usually every few years

Good entrance mats and daily dust mopping slow down wear a great deal, because they stop sand reaching the floor.

Frequently asked questions

Can scratched marble be repaired?

In most cases, yes. Light scratches are removed by honing and polishing. Deep scratches need diamond grinding first.

How long does marble polishing take?

It depends on the area and its condition. A villa floor may take one to three days. A large lobby is usually done in sections over several nights.

Can we use the floor straight after polishing?

Usually yes, once it is dry. After sealing, we advise keeping water and spills off the floor for the first day.

What should we use to clean marble every day?

A pH-neutral cleaner made for stone. Avoid vinegar, lemon, bleach and general bathroom cleaners, because they etch marble.

Do you also polish granite and terrazzo?

Yes. We treat marble, granite, terrazzo and other natural stone.
