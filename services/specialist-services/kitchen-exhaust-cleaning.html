Grease builds up fast in a busy kitchen. Why kitchen exhaust cleaning matters

Every time you cook, grease and smoke rise into the hood. Filters catch some of it. The rest coats the inside of the hood, the ductwork and the exhaust fan. Over time this layer gets thicker and harder to remove.

A dirty exhaust system can cause real problems:

  • Grease in the ducts is a fire risk, as it can catch light from heat or flames below
  • Airflow drops, so the kitchen gets hotter and smokier
  • The fan works harder and can wear out sooner
  • Smells and dripping grease become more likely
  • Inspections and audits can raise it as an issue

In Qatar, kitchens often run long hours and the outdoor heat adds to the load on ventilation. Keeping the system clean helps the kitchen stay safer and more comfortable for the people working in it.

What's included

We look at your system first, then plan the work. A typical clean covers:

  • Inspection of the hood, filters, ducts, access panels and exhaust fan
  • Covering cooking equipment and nearby surfaces to protect them
  • Removing and degreasing the hood filters
  • Cleaning the inside and outside of the canopy hood
  • Degreasing the ductwork through existing access points
  • Cleaning the exhaust fan housing and blades where they can be reached
  • Cleaning up the work area once the job is done
  • A short report with notes on anything that needs attention, such as damaged panels or areas with no access

If parts of the ductwork cannot be reached, we tell you and explain the options.

How often should kitchen exhausts be cleaned?

It depends on how much and what kind of cooking you do. As a general guide:

  • Heavy use, such as grills, fryers, woks and charcoal cooking: every one to three months
  • Moderate use, such as most restaurants and hotel kitchens: every three to six months
  • Light use, such as small cafés, staff pantries and some schools: every six to twelve months

Filters usually need cleaning much more often, sometimes weekly. During the site visit we can suggest a schedule that matches how your kitchen works.

Frequently asked questions

How much does kitchen exhaust hood cleaning cost in Qatar?

It depends on the size of the hood, the length of the ductwork, the number of fans and how much grease has built up. We offer a free site visit and then send a written quote, so the cost is clear before we start.

Do we need to close the kitchen during the cleaning?

Cooking has to stop while we work on the system. To keep disruption low, most kitchens book the clean overnight, early in the morning or on a day they are closed.

How long does the cleaning take?

A small kitchen with one hood can often be done in a few hours. Larger systems with long duct runs may take a full night or more than one visit. We give you a time estimate with the quote.

Is kitchen duct cleaning different from hood cleaning?

Yes. Hood cleaning covers the canopy and filters you can see. Duct cleaning goes further into the ductwork and up to the fan, where hidden grease collects. We usually recommend both.

Are the cleaning products safe for a food area?

We use degreasers suited to commercial kitchens, protect cooking equipment while we work and rinse surfaces afterwards. The kitchen is left clean and ready for your team to use.
