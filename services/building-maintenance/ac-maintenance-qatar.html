Why AC maintenance matters in Qatar

In Qatar, air conditioning is not a comfort extra. It runs for most of the year and often all day in summer. That puts real strain on every part of the system.

Dust and sand clog filters and coils. Drain lines block and leak. Fans and motors wear faster under constant load. When a system is not maintained, it works harder, cools less and costs more to run. A small fault left alone in spring can become a full breakdown in July, when it is hardest to arrange repairs.

Regular maintenance helps you:

  • Keep rooms at a steady, comfortable temperature
  • Improve indoor air quality for staff, tenants and families
  • Spot worn parts before they fail
  • Reduce energy use from dirty or struggling equipment
  • Extend the working life of your AC systems

Systems we maintain

We maintain a wide range of cooling systems found in Doha buildings:

  • Split units: wall-mounted, ceiling and cassette types in villas, apartments and offices
  • Ducted units: concealed units serving several rooms
  • Fan coil units (FCUs): common in towers and commercial buildings on chilled water
  • Air handling units (AHUs): larger units serving offices, malls, schools and public areas
  • Chillers: air-cooled and water-cooled plant supplying chilled water to the building

What planned preventive maintenance includes

The exact scope depends on your systems and is agreed after the site visit. A typical visit can include:

  • Cleaning or replacing filters
  • Cleaning coils, fins, blowers and drain pans
  • Flushing and checking drain lines for blockages and leaks
  • Checking refrigerant levels and pressures
  • Inspecting belts, bearings, fans and motors
  • Checking electrical connections, controls and thermostats
  • Measuring supply and return air temperatures
  • Recording findings and recommending any repairs

After each visit you receive a short report showing what was done and anything that needs attention.

How often should AC units be serviced?

In Qatar's climate, most systems benefit from more frequent attention than in cooler countries. As a general guide:

  • Split units in homes: every three to four months, with filter cleaning more often during dusty periods
  • Offices and shops: monthly or quarterly, depending on use
  • FCUs, AHUs and chillers: a planned schedule set around the equipment and the building's needs

We recommend a schedule after seeing your systems.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AC maintenance contract cost in Doha?

It depends on the number and type of units, their condition and how often you want visits. We offer a free site visit and send a written quote, so the cost is clear before you agree.

When should I service my AC in Qatar?

Just before summer, usually in March or April. A full check before the heat arrives helps avoid breakdowns when you need cooling most.

How long does a service visit take?

A single split unit is usually quick. Larger sites with FCUs, AHUs or chillers take longer and may be spread over several visits.

Do we need to switch the AC off during maintenance?

Each unit is switched off while it is being worked on. We plan the order of work so the rest of the building stays cool where possible.

Why is my AC leaking water?

The most common cause is a blocked drain line or dirty drain pan. Regular cleaning of the drain system usually prevents it.
