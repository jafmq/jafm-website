Planned Preventive Maintenance (PPM) Contracts in Qatar

Most building breakdowns can be prevented. A planned preventive maintenance (PPM) contract checks and services your building's systems on a fixed schedule, so problems are found early and fixed before they stop your building working. JAFM Qatar provides PPM contracts for offices, government buildings, schools, residential towers, compounds and villas across Qatar. We have worked in facility management since 1977 and hold ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification, certified by TNV.

Why PPM matters in Qatar

In Qatar's summer, air conditioning runs almost non-stop, and a breakdown quickly affects staff, residents and equipment. Dust clogs filters and coils, heat strains pumps and electrical panels, and water systems need regular checks. Planned maintenance catches these problems early. It also lowers electricity use, extends equipment life and keeps your building ready for inspections.

What a PPM contract covers

  • Air conditioning: split units, fan coil units, chillers, air handling units and pumps
  • Low-voltage electrical and lighting, including emergency lighting tests
  • Plumbing, drainage and water pumps
  • Water tanks, including cleaning and disinfection
  • Fire alarm and firefighting system checks
  • CCTV maintenance
  • Swimming pools
  • Minor civil, carpentry and painting works

The scope is agreed with you. A contract can cover a single system, such as air conditioning, or the whole building.

How it works

  1. Asset survey. We walk the building with you and list every system and its condition.
  2. PPM programme. We build a maintenance calendar for each system, with shutdown windows agreed with you.
  3. Scheduled visits. Technicians carry out each task on time, with a checklist for every asset.
  4. Reactive repairs. Faults are reported to one point of contact, logged as work orders and closed with your signature.
  5. Monthly report. You receive a report showing what was done, what is due and anything that needs your decision.

Typical PPM frequencies

AC filtersMonthly in summer, every 1 to 2 months in winter
AC coils and drain pansEvery 3 to 6 months
Chillers and air handling unitsMonthly and quarterly checks
Water pumps and booster setsMonthly
Water tanksCleaned and disinfected at least once or twice a year
Emergency lightingMonthly tests and a yearly full test
Fire alarm and firefighting systemsAs required by Qatar Civil Defence and the system's maintenance schedule

Exact frequencies depend on the equipment, the manufacturer's guidance and how the building is used. They are set out in your PPM programme.

  • Temporary repairs clearly labelled, with a date for the permanent fix
  • A monthly PPM report you can keep for inspections and audits

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between preventive and reactive maintenance?

Preventive maintenance is scheduled in advance to stop faults happening. Reactive maintenance fixes faults after they happen. A good contract combines both.

Can a PPM contract cover only air conditioning?

Yes. A contract can cover a single system or the whole building, and can be extended later.

Do you provide a report after each visit?

Every work order is signed by you, and you receive a monthly PPM report showing completed tasks, upcoming tasks and anything that needs a decision.

Can maintenance be combined with cleaning?

Yes. Combining maintenance with cleaning and other soft services gives you one provider and one point of contact for the whole building.

How do we report a fault between visits?

Faults are reported to one point of contact, logged as a work order and attended according to the response times in your contract.
