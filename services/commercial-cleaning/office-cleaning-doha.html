Why regular office cleaning matters

Offices get busy. Desks, keyboards, phones and door handles are touched all day. Pantries and washrooms are used by everyone. In Qatar, fine dust also finds its way inside, even with the windows closed, and settles on surfaces, vents and floors.

Regular cleaning helps you:

  • Keep shared areas hygienic for staff and visitors
  • Reduce dust, which matters for people with allergies
  • Make a good first impression on clients
  • Look after carpets, floors and furniture so they last longer
  • Let your team focus on their work, not on tidying up

How often should an office be cleaned?

Most offices in Doha need some cleaning every working day, especially washrooms, pantries and bins. Desks, floors and high-touch points also benefit from daily attention in a busy office.

Smaller or quieter offices may only need cleaning two or three times a week. Deep cleaning, such as carpet shampooing and floor polishing, is often done monthly or every few months. During the site visit we can suggest a schedule that fits your space and how many people use it.

Frequently asked questions

How much do office cleaning services cost in Qatar?

It depends on the size of the office, how often you need cleaning and what tasks are included. We offer a free site visit and then send you a written quote, so you can see the cost before you decide.

Can you clean outside office hours?

Yes. Many offices prefer cleaning early in the morning, in the evening or at weekends so work is not disturbed. We agree the timing with you when we set the schedule.

Do you bring cleaning materials and supplies?

This is agreed in the quote. We can arrange the cleaning materials and tools needed for the job, and washroom supplies such as soap and paper can be included if you wish.

How long does an office clean take?

It depends on the size of the space and the tasks involved. A small office may take an hour or two, while a full floor or a deep clean takes longer. We give you an estimate after the site visit.

Can you do a one-off deep clean?

Yes. We can do a one-off deep clean, for example before you move in, after a renovation or before an important event. You can also add regular cleaning afterwards if it suits you.
