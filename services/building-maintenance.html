Facility management services

A building only works when its systems do. We look after your building's systems with planned preventive maintenance and fast reactive repairs, and combine them with our soft services so you have one provider and one point of contact.

What's included

  • Planned preventive maintenance (PPM) and reactive repairs
  • Air conditioning: split units, fan coil units, chillers, air handling units and pumps
  • Low-voltage electrical and lighting by licensed technicians, including emergency lighting tests
  • Plumbing and drainage
  • Water tanks
  • Fire alarm and firefighting system checks
  • BMS operators
  • CCTV maintenance
  • Swimming pools
  • Minor civil, carpentry and painting works

How we deliver

  • A PPM programme agreed with you, including shutdown windows
  • Every work order signed by you, and a monthly PPM report
  • Permit-to-work and lockout/tagout for higher-risk work
  • Temporary repairs clearly labelled, with a date for the permanent repair

How it works

  1. Asset survey. We walk the building with you and list its systems: air conditioning, electrical, plumbing, fire systems, pumps, pools and so on, noting their condition.
  2. PPM programme. We build a planned preventive maintenance calendar for each system, with shutdown windows agreed with you.
  3. Reactive repairs. Faults are reported to one point of contact, logged as work orders and closed with your signature.
  4. Monthly reporting. You receive a monthly PPM report showing what was done, what is due and anything that needs your decision.

Why planned maintenance matters in Qatar

In Qatar's summer, air conditioning runs almost non-stop, and a breakdown quickly affects staff, guests and equipment. Regular filter cleaning, coil cleaning and checks catch problems early. They also lower electricity use and help the equipment last longer. The same applies to pumps, water tanks and fire systems: problems are cheaper to prevent than to fix.

Buildings we maintain

Offices, government buildings, schools, residential towers and compounds, villas, hotels and industrial facilities.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between hard and soft services?

Hard services cover the building's systems, such as air conditioning, electrical, plumbing and fire systems. Soft services cover cleaning, pest control and support staff. We provide both, so you have one provider.

How often should air conditioning be serviced in Qatar?

Split units and fan coil units usually need filter cleaning monthly and a full service every three to six months, more often in dusty locations.

Do you handle emergency breakdowns?

Yes. Reactive repairs are part of the service, and urgent faults are prioritised.

Can you take over maintenance of an existing building?

Yes. We start with a survey of the current condition of the systems, then agree a PPM programme and a handover plan with you.

Do you provide BMS operators?

Yes. We provide BMS operators to monitor the building's systems and raise work orders when something needs attention.
