About us

A Qatari facility management company since 1977

Jassim & Abdulaal Facilities Management (JAFM) was founded in Qatar on 1 February 1977.

We provide cleaning, hospitality staff and facility management to government bodies, hospitals, schools, banks, energy companies, embassies and private businesses, either as separate services or combined under a single contract.

Certified management systems

JAFM is certified by TNV to three international standards:

  • ISO 9001: Quality management
  • ISO 14001: Environmental management
  • ISO 45001: Occupational health and safety

How we train our people

  • A two-stage induction: first to the company, then to the site
  • New staff work alongside an experienced colleague until their supervisor signs them off
  • Mandatory safety training, including fire, first aid, heat stress, manual handling, PPE and safe chemical use
  • Training given in each worker's own language, using practical demonstration

Our vision

To be the leading facilities management company in the GCC, trusted for the reliability and efficiency of our service and the satisfaction of our clients.

Our mission

To deliver facility services that meet our clients' standards every day, and to keep improving them through better training, sound procedures and the right technology.
