About us

A Qatari facility management company since 1977

Jassim & Abdulaal Facilities Management (JAFM) was founded in Doha in 1977 with a vision to become the leading facility management company in Qatar.

Over 49 years we have worked across government, education, healthcare, hospitality and commercial buildings. That experience has shaped how we recruit, train and supervise our teams, and how we plan and check our work so that clients receive the same high standard every day.

Today we provide cleaning, building maintenance, pest control and office support staff, either as separate services or combined under a single facility management contract.

Certified management systems

JAFM is certified by the United Kingdom Accreditation Service (UKAS) to three international standards:

  • ISO 9001: Quality management
  • ISO 45001: Occupational health and safety
  • ISO 14001: Environmental management

Our vision

To be the leading and most trusted facility management company in Qatar.

Our mission

To deliver safe, consistent and high-quality facility services through trained people, proven procedures and long-term partnerships with our clients.
